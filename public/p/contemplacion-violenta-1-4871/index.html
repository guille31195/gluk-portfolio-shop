About the work

VIOLENTA holds the simultaneous presence of beauty and violence, refusing to let either redeem, justify, or erase the other. Neither condition cancels the other. Tenderness, danger, memory, longing, and loss coexist in the same frame.

Like oral histories and folk songs passed down, the story survives only through fragments, arriving too late, or too early, to witness the event itself. What remains is an atmosphere charged with possibility: the viewer becomes investigator, witness, and storyteller at once, in the space where attraction and threat, care and aggression, hold without resolution.

Caring for your painting

Light

Keep it out of direct sunlight. Strong light fades pigment over time.

Air

Hang it away from heat sources, kitchens and damp walls. Steady room conditions are best for oil on canvas.

Cleaning

Dust gently with a dry, soft brush or cloth. Don't use water or cleaning products on the surface.
