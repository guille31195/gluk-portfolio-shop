Toxic Arts

Oil, ink and code, put in friction. A Caribbean painter from Caracas, working in Mexico City.

“I bend luxury until it fits our streets.”

Gluk

Toxic Arts shows work where it has energy: a former public toilet on Commercial Street, a garage. Gluk’s work lives in the same place. This month he painted a Ferrari on a truck tarpaulin and showed it in a working Manchester garage.

His aesthetic is both precise and streetwise: elegant without being domesticated. Oil paintings of Caribbean street life, tattooed hides, and new media that has reached the Louvre.
